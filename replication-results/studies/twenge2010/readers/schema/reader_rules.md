# Reader-stage rules (extracted verbatim in substance from the project workflow)

Source: project instructions/workflow.md section 2, instructions/supplements.md and prompts/reader.md. Provided identically to both readers.

- Read the main article and every permitted supplement in the frozen publication packet. Cite document ID and PDF page (and table/section) for every claim and choice. Report contradictions between main text and supplements; do not silently privilege one. Do not infer that no supplement exists because none is in the packet; report gaps.
- Do not access raw data, other readers, prior studies, results or scoring criteria. Do not fit models. Published-table forensic calculations remain separate from raw-data estimation and are sealed with targets.
- Claim map: each hypothesis or stated finding, supporting table/column/row, estimand, population, scale and tier. Tiers distinguish focal estimates, identification/inference diagnostics, robustness, descriptives and nuisance controls.
- Sealed targets: coefficient or effect size, published SE, exact p or stars/inequality, alpha, tail/direction, N, model and source locator. Missing values remain missing. No-star entries are not exact p values.
- Target-free implementation manifest: ordered sample steps, variable definitions, source keys, estimator, covariance methods, software defaults, missingness and inference fields. Include an opaque paper ID; keep published N in a separate machine-evaluated branch outside implementer-visible targets.
- Specification space: axes/levels for genuine open decisions, a closest-described origin, sourced equivalent/non-equivalent/uncertain labels and rationales. Prioritize sample construction and variable coding, then fixed effects/clustering, then estimator. Alternatives must have an article or methodological-literature basis. Explicit control or item lists cannot be changed within the equivalent set. Distinct reported outcomes are not automatically interchangeable specifications.
- Wider space and documentation assessment: default/common/rare and justified/unjustified choices, structural counts and a holistic unusualness rating. These describe the documented choices and never determine a replication verdict. The wider grid is off unless explicitly included before execution.
- Inference fields: observation/sampling/assignment units, clusters, native SE method/software, first-stage strength where applicable; documentation scores for specificity, precision and exhaustiveness; item-specific unavailable-source and vintage fallbacks.
- Forensic observations based solely on the published record: consistency of effect size/SE/test statistic/p/stars, N/df, table identities and transformations. Use whole rounding intervals before declaring an internal mismatch. Forensic findings are separate records, not verdicts.
- Do not choose an interpretation by numerical agreement with anything. Do not invent alternatives to meet a quota or silently correct article values using memory. Printed observations and tables are not empirical data; they are only targets.
