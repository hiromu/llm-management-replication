Amendment §A5 requires industry collinearity to be resolved before checking residual degrees of freedom. `code/k7_lib.R:523–526` instead stops when the sample size is no greater than the number of non-industry columns, before the collinearity checks and industry drops at `code/k7_lib.R:527–536`. This omits collinear industry drops from the failure record and can report a degrees-of-freedom failure without identifying collinear non-industry terms as §A5 requires.
2. Amendment §A6 requires dropped-column information to be recorded even when a fit later fails. For a failed restricted-model refit, `code/k7_lib.R:718–724` retains only `rfit$error` and discards `rfit$dropped`; `code/run_grid.R:89–91` likewise writes only that error to `run_info.json`. Any industry columns dropped before that restricted refit fails are therefore absent from the outputs.
3. Amendment §A6 requires one failed influence row per estimation-sample record when the original model fails. When that sample is known to be empty, `code/k7_lib.R:743–749` replaces the empty identifier vector with `NA` and emits one influence row despite there being no record to delete. `code/run_grid.R:104–108` also lists this nonexistent deletion as a failure in `run_info.json`.
