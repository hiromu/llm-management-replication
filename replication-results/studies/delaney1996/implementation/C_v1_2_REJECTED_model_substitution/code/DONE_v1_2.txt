PAPER_K7 grid implementation, repair round v1.2 complete (2026-10-28).

Changes (confined to regression.py and pipeline.py):
- Checker item 1 (A5/A10): regression._check_non_dummy_columns no longer raises a residual-df error
  at the dummy-resolution stage. A rank-deficient non-dummy design, including one with no more rows
  than non-dummy columns, is reported with its all-zero / collinear reason; the residual-df check is
  left to fit_linear, after the permitted industry-dummy drops. Applies to reported models, nested
  restricted models and every influence refit.
- Checker item 2 (A6): pipeline.influence_rows emits one influence row per estimation-sample record
  (term NA) when the original model fails, via the new _orig_failed_row, instead of one row per
  record per term; and emits no row for an empty estimation sample (the stray deleted_V1 = NA row is
  gone). Per-deletion failures inside a successful model still list every term.
- Checker item 3 (section 6): pipeline.run_spec reports k and n_industry_dummies from the resolved
  column names when the fit fails after a successful dummy resolution; _stat_row accepts them without
  a successful fit. Both stay NA only when resolution failed or the sample was empty.
- run_grid.py: implementation_version bumped to v1.2.
