# Reader-stage rules

These rules are taken from the study's workflow and supplement instructions and apply to both readers equally.

- Read the assigned main article and every supplement in the frozen packet. Cite document ID (MAIN or S1), PDF page and table or section for every claim and choice.
- Do not access raw data, other readers' work, prior studies, results or scoring criteria. Do not fit models. Do not choose an interpretation by numerical agreement.
- Produce: a claim map; a sealed targets file; a target-free implementation manifest; a specification space; a documentation assessment with a wider-space description; inference fields; forensic observations based only on the published record.
- Claim map: each hypothesis or stated finding, its supporting table/column/row, estimand, population, scale and tier. Tiers distinguish focal estimates, identification/inference diagnostics, robustness, descriptives and nuisance controls.
- Sealed targets: coefficient, published SE, exact p or stars/inequality, alpha, tail/direction, N, model and source locator. Missing values remain missing. No-star entries are not exact p values.
- Manifest: ordered sample steps, variable definitions, source keys, estimator, fixed or random effects, covariance methods, software defaults, missingness and inference fields. Use the opaque paper ID. Keep published N in the sealed_n_branch field only.
- Specification space: axes and levels for genuine open decisions, a closest-described origin, labels equivalent / non_equivalent / uncertain with rationales. Prioritize sample construction and variable coding, then fixed effects/clustering, then estimator. Alternatives need an article or methodological-literature basis. Explicitly listed controls cannot be changed within the equivalent set. Distinct reported outcomes are not automatically interchangeable specifications. Do not invent alternatives to meet a quota.
- Wider space and documentation assessment: default/common/rare and justified/unjustified choices, structural counts and a holistic unusualness rating. These describe the documented choices only.
- Inference fields: observation, sampling and assignment units, clusters, native SE method and software; documentation scores for specificity, precision and exhaustiveness; item-specific unavailable-source and vintage fallbacks.
- Forensic observations: consistency of coefficient/SE/test statistic/p/stars, N and df, table identities and transformations. Use whole rounding intervals before declaring an internal mismatch. Forensic findings are separate records, not verdicts.
- Supplements: printed numbers in the article and supplement are comparison targets only, never data. Report contradictions between the main text and the supplement; do not silently privilege one. Do not reconstruct removed code. Do not infer that no supplement means none exists.
- Do not silently correct article values from memory. Ask the coordinator in reader_notes.md about missing inputs.
