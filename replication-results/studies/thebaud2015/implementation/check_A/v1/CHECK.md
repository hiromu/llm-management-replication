7. Manifest §8, sample counts and SHA-256 sample_hash: `run.R` lines 641–642 and 651–657 leave n_countries, n_surveys, and sample_hash missing for an empty estimation sample, rather than recording zero countries, zero surveys, and the SHA-256 of the empty joined ID sequence.
