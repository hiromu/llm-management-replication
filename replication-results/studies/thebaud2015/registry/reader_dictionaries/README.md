# Reader source dictionaries (summary-free)

These files describe the empirical sources registered for this study. They give source identity, definitions, variable names, codes, units and coverage expressed only as which countries and years are present. They contain no observations, counts, means, frequencies, distributions or other observed values. Raw data files are not part of the reader packet.

| File | Sources described |
|---|---|
| `icpsr20320_gem_aps.md` | GEM Adult Population Survey harmonized file 1998-2017 (ICPSR 20320 V6): all 97 variables with labels, codes and missing-value codes; country-years present; years in which key items have responses |
| `oecd_sources.md` | OECD part-time incidence, employment protection (EPL), harmonised unemployment, LFS participation/unemployment rates, union density, SOCX family spending, OECD Family Database spreadsheets (leave, childcare spending, enrolment, fertility, gender employment) |
| `leave_policy_documents.md` | CEPR parental-leave reports (Ray 2008; Ray, Gornick and Schmitt 2008) and archived ILO TRAVAIL maternity-protection country pages (2009 and 2011 editions) |
| `undp_worldbank_gem_sources.md` | UNDP Human Development Reports 2004-2010, World Bank income classifications, Doing Business historical data, WDI GDP per capita PPP, original GEM 2001-2002 annual public files; sources not acquired |

Registry status at preparation (see `../source_registry.json`): available - OECD sources, World Bank sources, GEM 2001-2002 original files; partially available - ICPSR 20320 (status item absent in 2001-2002; work status and education absent for France 2003), CEPR reports (journal version not obtained; 21 countries), ILO pages (not all countries/editions captured), UNDP HDRs (GDP index and female-managers share printed only in 2004-2009 reports); unavailable - ISSP 2002 (login required), Charles and Bradley 2009 field segregation (no public dataset).

All non-GEM country-level files are current releases downloaded in October 2026; the article used releases available around 2012-2013.
