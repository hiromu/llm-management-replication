# Publication packet

Before the readers run, freeze packet/publication_packet.json in the study folder. List each document, its hash, order, version, identity check and known gaps. Both readers receive the identical packet.

The study's CLAUDE.md lists the documents: the article, its online appendix, and the deposited documentation readers may receive. Readers use appendices only for the study's in-scope results.

Do not treat a documentary appendix as data. Numerical results in any document are sealed comparison targets or publication diagnostics, never empirical inputs. If a document is added after initial reading, version the packet and give both readers the same chance to reread. Preserve initial outputs and initial agreement statistics.

Implementers and pre-execution checkers never receive the packet.
