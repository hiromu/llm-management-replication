1. R02.no_special: the RSSD9425 special-purpose exclusion is not implemented at all; `code/analysis.R:69` unconditionally raises `unavailable` on the claim that the dictionary gives no codes, which cannot be verified here because `docs/` is not present.
2. R06.rck_days: the level specifies a calendar-day weighted mean of the four quarterly 3368 values, but `code/analysis.R:176-178` day-weights whatever subset of quarters passes the active R05 coverage rule (two or three quarters at O_A) instead of requiring all four.
3. R26.centered (M3_SQ): the centered regressor is defined as SD minus its sample mean, but for M3_SQ `code/analysis.R:444` builds the column SD² − c² (equivalently z² + 2cz) rather than the centered square (SD − c)², so the fitted design is not the centered basis the level describes.
