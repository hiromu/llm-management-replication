10. Manifest §2 S5: Market growth uses the preceding available benchmark row rather than explicitly matching calendar year t-1, so a missing benchmark year causes growth to span a gap (`code/analysis.R:626`, `code/analysis.R:628`).

15. Manifest §6: Construction logs omit yearly attrition counts for duplicate removal and bank-years lacking Q4 records, since S1 counts precede deduplication and S2 counts begin after Q4 filtering (`code/analysis.R:175`, `code/analysis.R:187`, `code/analysis.R:202`, `code/analysis.R:225`).
