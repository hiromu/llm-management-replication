## Forensic observations and contradictions

The printed nested SSE/F/df arithmetic is compatible with rounding; Table 1's squared-variable moment identity is also compatible. The coarse M3 quadratic SE makes a point-estimate t-versus-F comparison misleading: full rounding intervals allow agreement. Coefficient and correlation star checks use complete rounding intervals under explicitly conditional conventional tests. None of those checks identifies implementation choices or supplies empirical input (DOC01 p12–13).

The statement that a bank's deviation is zero if and only if **all firms** have the same strategy does not follow from its equation. A focal bank can equal every strategy mean while others differ. If all firms are identical, the displayed SD denominators are zero. This is a logical inconsistency and an omitted zero-variance convention, not proof of an estimation error (DOC01 p10 equation and immediately following prose).

The h statement says "less than 1.28 (N.S.)", without absolute-value notation. That bound alone does not exclude a large negative statistic; missing exact values prevent checking the inference (DOC01 p11). The reported largest M3 condition number exceeds its cited concern threshold, which the author acknowledges while relying on other checks; do not convert this into a numerical contradiction (DOC01 p13).

The p16 discussion says larger amounts of **similarity** reduced performance and then invokes loss of legitimacy, whereas the theory connects legitimacy challenges to too much differentiation. This may be a wording slip, but it is preserved without correction (compare DOC01 p8 and p16). Relative-ROA centering is not equivalent to a full year fixed-effects specification. The blanket statement that GLS negates R-squared interpretation is overbroad, but no R-squared value is printed to audit (DOC01 p9, p11).
